# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Orrery welcomes contributions from people with different backgrounds and
levels of experience. We expect everyone who participates in the project to
help keep it respectful, clear and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Explain technical disagreements with evidence and assume good faith.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing credentials, customer data or private
  operational details.
- Accept review feedback and keep discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate
disruption, sexualized attention, and publishing private or sensitive
information are not permitted.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub's private reporting channel
when it is enabled, or through a private channel provided by a maintainer.
Please include links or a concise description and avoid posting sensitive
details publicly. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation or
take other action needed to protect participants and the project.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is representing Orrery
or the project community.
